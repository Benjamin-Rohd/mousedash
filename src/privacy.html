Privacy Policy

MouseDash is a free browser game. This policy explains what information the site handles and why.

What is collected

Anonymous player ID. When you load the game, an anonymous identifier is stored by your browser. It is not linked to a name, email address, or any other personal detail. It exists to associate a run with the player who started it.

Scores and messages. If you set a high score, your score and any message you choose to submit are stored and displayed publicly on the site. Messages are visible to everyone. Do not include personal information. Scores and messages are deleted periodically when the leaderboard resets.

Server logs. Anonymous usage data such as IP addresses, browser type, device information, referring URLs, and interaction patterns may be collected to maintain site security, diagnose technical issues, and improve the user experience.

Third-party services

Google reCAPTCHA is used to verify that requests come from a real browser rather than an automated script. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information and behavioral data for this purpose. It is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, and server functions.

Cloudflare hosts the site and provides aggregate, cookieless analytics. No cookies are set for analytics purposes.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Your browser stores an anonymous authentication identifier locally so the game works across page loads. reCAPTCHA may set cookies as part of its abuse-detection function.

Your rights

Because no personal information is collected beyond an anonymous identifier, there is typically nothing to request or delete. Clearing your browser's site data removes the identifier. If you have submitted a message you want removed, contact the address below.

For users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation. Data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest — operating, securing, and improving the site. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your personal data
  • Receive a copy of your data in a structured format
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

As described above, no personal information is collected beyond an anonymous identifier, so in most cases there is nothing to access or delete. To exercise any of these rights, contact the address below.

For California residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act provides you with specific rights regarding your personal information. You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising these rights

Personal information is not sold, as that term is defined by the CCPA. To exercise your rights, contact the address below.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date below. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
